About the App

BesideHer is a pregnancy companion for first-time dads. It tracks your partner's pregnancy week by week, provides practical tips, checklists, and weekly reminders to help you stay present and prepared.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I set my partner's due date? Open the app and go to Settings. Tap "Due Date" to update it at any time.
  • How do weekly notifications work? Once you enable notifications during setup, BesideHer sends you a reminder each week matched to your partner's current pregnancy week. You can turn them on or off in Settings.
  • Does the app require an internet connection? No. All content is built into the app and works fully offline.
  • Is my data shared with anyone? No. BesideHer stores your due date only on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
  • How do I reset my checklist progress? Deleting and reinstalling the app will clear all local data, including checklist completions.
