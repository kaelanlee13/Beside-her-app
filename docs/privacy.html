Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Overview

BesideHer is a pregnancy companion app designed for expectant fathers. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

BesideHer does not collect any personal data. All information you enter — including your partner's due date, notification preferences, checklist progress, and bookmarked tips — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

BesideHer does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services. We do not share any data with third parties.

Notifications

If you enable weekly reminders, BesideHer uses Apple's local notification system to schedule notifications on your device. These notifications are generated entirely on-device and no notification data is sent to external servers.

Children's Privacy

BesideHer is intended for use by adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future update, the updated policy will be made available at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
